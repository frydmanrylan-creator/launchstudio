Questions

Before you start

What exactly do I get for $250?

A custom one-page website with up to five sections, such as an intro, services, service area, about you and contact. It's designed for your business, works on phones and includes one round of revisions. You receive the finished site files.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, services, service area and how customers should contact you. A logo and photos help but aren't required. If you don't have photos, I can suggest licensed stock images. You approve all wording before delivery.

How do revisions work?

After you see the preview, send one list with all the changes you'd like to the text, images or layout, within the agreed scope. I apply them together. Further changes or new sections can be quoted separately.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

Yes, to put the site online under your own address. They're paid directly to the provider you choose, not to me. I'll explain the options, any recurring costs and the publishing steps before you decide.

Who owns the website?

After final payment you receive the complete site files. You can host them with any provider you choose, and you're not locked into working with me.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project and my current schedule. I confirm a specific delivery date by email before you pay anything, and the date assumes I have your content by then.

What happens before I pay?

You send a brief and I reply with the proposed scope and a delivery date. You only pay the $125 deposit once you've agreed to both. If anything changes the scope later, we agree on it before I do the work.

Can you add online booking or a store?

This package is an informational page. I can link to booking, ordering or review tools you already use. Custom checkout, accounts or booking systems need a separate quote.
